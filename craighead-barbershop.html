Our Story

A Nashville Classic on Jefferson Street

Craighead Barber Shop is a historic landmark on Nashville's storied Jefferson Street — a neighborhood institution where great cuts and good conversation have always gone hand in hand.

Owner Joe is known throughout the community as one of the friendliest, most professional barbers around. Whether you're a regular or walking in for the first time, you'll be treated like family — no long waits, fair prices, and a welcoming atmosphere every single visit.

We're proud to be part of Nashville's history and to keep the classic barbershop tradition alive.
